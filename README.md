### ETF DESCRIPTION

The Invesco QQQ Trust ETF (ticker: QQQ) is a passive index fund designed to track the investment results of the Nasdaq-100 Index®. Launched on March 10, 1999, and managed by Invesco, this fund offers exposure to 100 of the largest non-financial companies listed on the Nasdaq Stock Market. The ETF boasts an impressive Asset Under Management (AUM) of $303.14 billion and carries a competitive expense ratio of 0.20%.

QQQ predominantly focuses on U.S. large-cap growth equities within the technology sector, which constitutes 52% of its portfolio. Other key sectors include Communication Services (16%) and Consumer Cyclical (13%). Top holdings include Apple (9.05%), Microsoft (7.98%), NVIDIA (7.91%), Amazon (5.66%), and Broadcom (4.06%), reflecting a strong emphasis on technology and innovation-driven companies.

The Nasdaq-100 Index®, managed by Nasdaq, employs a market-capitalization weighting methodology and is rebalanced quarterly. It excludes financial companies and prioritizes liquidity and market capitalization as selection criteria.

This ETF distributes dividends quarterly, with the next ex-dividend date scheduled for March 24, 2025, and a payout amount of $0.72 per share. Historical returns indicate robust performance, with a 10-year average annual return of 18.28% before taxes as of December 31, 2024.

The Invesco QQQ Trust ETF is an ideal choice for investors seeking diversified exposure to high-growth sectors like technology and communication services, with a focus on well-established, innovative companies. However, its concentration in these sectors makes it susceptible to volatility associated with market and economic shifts.
